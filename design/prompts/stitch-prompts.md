```text
App: Kitchen OS v3.0 — Professional Culinary Operations & Inventory Mobile App, for boutique winery chefs and line cooks. Mood: Swiss Kitchen Editorial, ultra-legible daylight, tactile precision, zero-clutter operational.
Platform: Cross-platform mobile web app, iPhone 15 393x852 portrait. Crisp daylight theme.

- Cotton Paper #F8F7F4 — primary app canvas background
- Crisp White #FFFFFF — row and structural block surface
