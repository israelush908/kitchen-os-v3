# Project Specification: Kitchen OS v3.0 — Dual-Mode Culinary & Operations Web App

## 1. Role & Objective
You are an expert Full-Stack Architect and UI/UX Product Designer. Your task is to build **Kitchen OS v3.0**, a responsive, bilingual-ready (Hebrew RTL interface, clean English code/keys) Web Application (PWA) for managing a winery visitor center kitchen (focused on cold-prep, assembly, cheese platters, bruschettas, salads, and dips).

The app must solve the visual clutter of complex spreadsheets by implementing **Progressive Disclosure** and providing two distinct operational modes connected to the same underlying data (Google Sheets API / Google Apps Script Backend or local JSON/REST sync):
1. **📱 Field Mode (Mobile-First View):** Designed for one-handed use while standing in walk-in fridges, storage, or prep stations (Inventory Counts, Daily Prep Checklists, Smart WhatsApp Ordering, Fast Waste Logging).
2. **🖥️ Office Mode (Desktop View):** Designed for seated management (Executive KPI Dashboard, Master-Detail Recipe & Food Cost Studio, Catalog & Par Level Management, Monthly Labor & SPLH Tracker).

---

## 2. Design System & UI/UX Principles
- **Directionality:** Strict RTL (`dir="rtl"`) for all UI layouts, tables, drawers, and inputs.
- **Aesthetic:** Quiet Luxury & Minimalist Operational UI. High contrast for kitchen readability, generous whitespace, zero visual noise.
- **Grid & Spacing:** Strict **8pt Grid System** (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px` padding/margins).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background Primary: `#FAFAFA` (Warm Off-White)
  - Surface / Cards: `#FFFFFF` with subtle `1px solid #EAEAEA` border
  - Text Primary: `#111111` (Deep Charcoal)
  - Text Secondary / Muted: `#666666`
  - Accent / Primary Action: `#1E1E1E` (Monochrome Black) or Deep Wine (`#5A1827`) for primary CTAs
  - Semantic Statuses:
    - Optimal (`תקין`): `#16A34A` (Soft Green badge)
    - Warning (`גבולי`): `#D97706` (Amber badge)
    - Critical / Shortage (`חוסר / חריגה`): `#DC2626` (Crimson badge)
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Never show raw formulas, secondary IDs, or financial metrics on operational field screens. Reveal recipe preparation instructions or BOM breakdowns only inside slide-over Drawers or Modals on demand.

## 3. Normalized Data Schema (Backend Structure)
Instead of side-by-side tables on a single sheet, structure the data layer into clean, normalized entities:

### 3.1 `DB_Config`
- `config_key` (String): `Target_FoodCost` (default `0.30`), `Target_LaborCost` (default `0.15`), `Employer_Cost_Multiplier` (default `1.30`), `VAT_Rate` (default `0.18`).
- `config_value` (Number/String)

### 3.2 `DB_Suppliers`
- `supplier_id` (String, PK, e.g., `SUP-01`)
- `supplier_name` (String, e.g., `אשי שוק`, `אקזוטיקה`, `אייס ישראל`)
- `category` (String, e.g., `ירקות`, `גבינות ומחלבה`, `קרח`)
- `contact_name` (String)
- `whatsapp_phone` (String, international format `9725...`)
- `delivery_days` (Array/String)
- `is_active` (Boolean)

### 3.3 `DB_Catalog` (Raw Ingredients)
- `item_id` (String, PK, e.g., `ITM-001`)
- `item_name` (String, e.g., `מנצ'גו כמהין`, `חסה קיסר`, `אננס`)
- `category` (Enum: `ירקות ופירות`, `גבינות ומחלבה`, `לחמים ומאפים`, `יבשים ושימורים`, `שתייה`, `אריזות ומתכלים`)
- `supplier_id` (String, FK -> `DB_Suppliers`)
- `stock_unit` (Enum: `ק"ג`, `ליטר`, `יח'`, `נספק`, `צרור`, `ארגז`)
- `unit_cost_excl_vat` (Number, ₪)
- `yield_loss_pct` (Number, 0 to 1, e.g., `0.10` for 10% peeling/trimming waste)
- `effective_cost` (Formula: `unit_cost_excl_vat / (1 - yield_loss_pct)`)
- `par_level_weekend` (Number - target stock for weekend service)
- `reorder_point` (Number - minimum threshold that triggers an order)
- `current_stock` (Number - dynamically updated from latest count or quick override)
- `in_count_form` (Boolean)
- `in_waste_form` (Boolean)
- `is_active` (Boolean)

### 3.4 `DB_Dishes` (Menu Items & Sub-Recipes)
- `dish_id` (String, PK)
- `dish_name` (String, e.g., `מטבל טחינה`, `פלטת גבינות זוגית`, `ברוסקטה סלק ולאבנה`)
- `dish_type` (Enum: `מתכון הכנה` [Sub-Recipe], `מנת תפריט` [Menu Dish])
- `category` (String: `פלטות וגבינות`, `מנות פתיחה ונשנושים`, `ברוסקטות וסלטים`, `מתכוני הכנה`, `שתייה`)
- `yield_portions` (Number - how many portions or liters/kg this recipe produces)
- `price_incl_vat` (Number, ₪ - `0` if `מתכון הכנה`)
- `price_excl_vat` (Formula: `price_incl_vat / (1 + VAT_Rate)`)
- `weekly_sales_qty` (Number - for Menu Engineering Matrix)
- `prep_instructions` (Text)

### 3.5 `DB_Recipe_Lines` (Bill of Materials - BOM)
- `line_id` (String, PK)
- `parent_dish_id` (String, FK -> `DB_Dishes`)
- `component_type` (Enum: `RAW_ITEM`, `SUB_RECIPE`)
- `component_id` (String, FK -> `DB_Catalog.item_id` OR `DB_Dishes.dish_id`)
- `quantity` (Number)
- `unit` (String)
- `line_cost` (Computed: `quantity * component.effective_cost`)
- `notes` (String)

### 3.6 `DB_Prep_Checklist` (Daily Station Prep)
- `prep_id` (String, PK)
- `station` (Enum: `פלטות וגבינות`, `ברוסקטות וסלטים`, `הכנות קרות`, `כללי`)
- `prep_item` (String, e.g., `מילוי קופסאות פלטה`, `סידור מגשי ענבים`, `גריסת פקאנים מסוכרים`)
- `frequency` (Enum: `יומי`, `לפני סופ"ש`, `שבועי`)
- `est_minutes` (Number)
- `done_today` (Boolean)
- `instructions` (String)
- `linked_dish_id` (Optional FK -> `DB_Dishes` to view recipe in a drawer)

### 3.7 `OPS_Manager_Tasks` (Back-Office Tasks)
- `task_id` (String, PK)
- `title` (String)
- `area` (String)
- `assignee` (String, default `ישראל`)
- `priority` (Enum: `גבוהה`, `רגילה`, `נמוכה`)
- `due_date` (Date)
- `status` (Enum: `לביצוע`, `בתהליך`, `הושלם`)
- `notes` (String)

### 3.8 Operational Logs (`LOG_Counts`, `LOG_Waste`, `LOG_Labor`)
- **`LOG_Counts`**: `count_id`, `timestamp`, `item_id`, `counted_qty`, `unit_cost_snapshot`, `count_value`, `reporter`
- **`LOG_Waste`**: `waste_id`, `timestamp`, `reporter`, `item_id`, `waste_qty`, `waste_reason` (`פג תוקף / קלקול`, `הכנת יתר`, `טעות בהכנה / שריפה`), `total_waste_cost`, `notes`
- **`LOG_Labor`**: `month_id` (e.g., `2026-10`), `sales_incl_vat`, `sales_excl_vat`, `service_hours`, `prep_hours`, `hourly_wages_gross`, `salaried_gross`, `employer_cost`, `labor_pct`, `splh` (`sales_excl_vat / total_hours`), `notes`

## 4. Application Architecture & Views

Include a global **Mode Switcher** in the top header: `[ 📱 מצב שטח (Field) | 🖥️ מצב משרד (Office) ]` (Auto-defaults to Field Mode on screens `< 768px` and Office Mode on `>= 768px`).

### MODE A: 📱 Field App (Mobile-First Interface)
Bottom Navigation Bar with 4 tabs:

1. **📦 ספירת מלאי (Inventory Count):**
   - Sticky top horizontal category pills (`הכל`, `גבינות ומחלבה`, `ירקות ופירות`, `לחמים ומאפים`, `יבשים`) + Search bar.
   - Clean item cards showing: `item_name`, `stock_unit` badge, and `Last Count` reference.
   - Large touch-friendly stepper (`-` / `[ input ]` / `+`) for entering current quantity.
   - Sticky bottom button: **"שמור ועדכן מלאי (X פריטים)"** -> updates `current_stock` and logs to `LOG_Counts`.

2. **🔪 הכנות וסרוויס (Station Prep):**
   - Segmented control by Station: `פלטות וגבינות` | `ברוסקטות וסלטים` | `הכנות קרות`.
   - Progress bar at top showing station readiness (e.g., `6/8 הושלמו - 45 דק' נותרו`).
   - Checklist cards with large checkboxes, `prep_item` title, and `est_minutes` badge.
   - Tapping the info icon on a card opens a bottom sheet / drawer with exact `instructions`, target weights, and sub-recipe ingredients if linked.

3. **📲 הזמנות רכש (Smart Orders):**
   - Automatically groups items where `current_stock <= reorder_point` by Supplier.
   - Displays Supplier Cards showing Supplier Name, Contact Name, and badge: `X פריטים בחוסר`.
   - Expanding a Supplier Card lists the recommended order quantities (`order_qty = par_level_weekend - current_stock`), with inline stepper to adjust quantities manually before sending.
   - Primary CTA per supplier: **"📲 שלח הזמנה בוואטסאפ"** -> generates a formatted Hebrew WhatsApp message (`https://wa.me/{whatsapp_phone}?text=...`) with date, item names, quantities, and units.

4. **🗑️ דיווח פחת (Quick Waste):**
   - 3-step fast entry card (takes < 5 seconds):
     1. Select Item (searchable chips/dropdown filtered by `in_waste_form = true`).
     2. Enter Quantity + Unit.
     3. Tap Reason Pill (`פג תוקף / קלקול` | `הכנת יתר` | `טעות בהכנה`).
   - Shows instant cost impact feedback and logs to `LOG_Waste`.

### MODE B: 🖥️ Office Back-Office (Desktop Interface)
Sidebar navigation with 5 modules:

1. **📊 מרכז שליטה (Executive Dashboard):**

     6. **משימות פתוחות לסרוויס/ניהול**
   - Actionable Insights panel below: Lists specific dishes exceeding target Food Cost and top 3 waste drivers this month.

2. **🍽️ סטודיו מתכונים והנדסת תפריט (Recipe & Food Cost Studio):**
   - **Master-Detail Split View** (eliminating side-by-side spreadsheet scrolling):
     - **Right Pane (Master List):** Filterable list of all Dishes & Sub-Recipes showing Name, Category, Food Cost %, Gross Profit `₪`, and Menu Matrix badge (`כוכב ⭐`, `סוס עבודה 🐴`, `חידה 🧩`, `כלב 🐕`).
     - **Left Pane (Detail Inspector):** Clicking any dish opens its full card: Yield, Pricing (incl./excl. VAT), interactive BOM Ingredients table (add/remove ingredients or sub-recipes, adjust quantities, see live `line_cost` and `Food_Cost_Pct` updates), and preparation instructions.

3. **🗂️ קטלוג, ספקים ותקנים (Catalog & Par Manager):**
   - Clean data table with inline editing for `unit_cost_excl_vat`, `yield_loss_pct`, `par_level_weekend`, and `reorder_point`.
   - Sub-tab for managing `DB_Suppliers` (WhatsApp numbers, contacts, delivery days).

4. **👥 בקרת לייבור ורווחיות (Labor & SPLH Tracker):**
   - Monthly table tracking Net Sales, Service Hours vs. Prep Hours, Hourly Wages, Salaried Chef cost, Employer Cost Multiplier (`1.30`), Actual Labor %, and SPLH (Sales Per Labor Hour).

5. **✅ משימות ניהול (Manager Tasks):**
   - Clean task list / Kanban for one-off operational tasks (e.g., menu tastings, event prep, supplier updates), kept completely separate from daily kitchen station checklists.
